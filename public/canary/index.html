BONDFIRE CANARY

Hosted infrastructure integrity statement.

The canary supplements Bondfire’s architecture. It does not replace it. Private organization content should already be encrypted before the hosted server stores it.

EXPIRED // NO CURRENT SIGNED STATEMENT

STATUS

Bondfire does not currently publish a human-signed OpenPGP canary in this repository. This page therefore does not claim a current canary. A future statement becomes current only after a human reviews the exact text, adds issue and expiration dates, signs it, and publishes the signature and fingerprint.

An expired or missing canary is not proof of a particular event.

Treat the absence of a current statement as a change in the information available to you, not as proof of any specific legal demand, compromise, or third-party action.

WHAT A SIGNED STATEMENT WILL COVER

  • The hosted Bondfire infrastructure remains under project control.
  • The private-data encryption model has not knowingly been intentionally bypassed or weakened.
  • Bondfire has not knowingly deployed software intended to provide a third party access to users’ private organization content.
  • Bondfire has not knowingly suffered a breach that provides unauthorized access to decrypted private organization content.
  • No claim will be made about user-held private keys that Bondfire does not possess.

The canary applies only to hosted Bondfire infrastructure. Self-hosted deployments are controlled by their respective operators.

HUMAN REVIEW CYCLE

Target review dates are February 1, May 1, August 1, and November 1. Future statements must never be generated or signed automatically. Each statement should include a contemporary public reference chosen during review to make pre-signing a stack of future statements harder.

VERIFICATION FORMAT

When the first canary is issued, this page and the raw text will publish: statement text, issued date, expiration date, OpenPGP signature, signing-key fingerprint, and an archive of previous statements.
