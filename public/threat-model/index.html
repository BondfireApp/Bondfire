THREAT MODEL

Know what the protection is for.

Bondfire narrows the amount of readable private organization data centralized in server infrastructure. It does not turn unsafe endpoints, unsafe members, or unsafe operational practices into safe ones.

DESIGNED TO HELP PROTECT AGAINST

  • Infrastructure operators casually reading private organization content from application storage.
  • A database exposure revealing plaintext private organization records where ciphertext-authoritative storage is used correctly.
  • Unauthorized users simply bypassing organization, module, or role boundaries through normal server API paths.
  • Unnecessary central storage of plaintext private organization data.

DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY PROTECT AGAINST

  • Compromised member devices or endpoint malware.
  • Malicious authorized members.
  • Screenshots, copied text, exports, or photography of a screen.
  • Stolen unlocked devices.
  • Weak passwords, session theft, or account takeover.
  • Exposed recovery material or encryption keys.
  • Someone intentionally publishing information that should have remained private.
  • Coercion.
  • Poor operational security.
  • Metadata exposure inherent in networked services.
  • Damage caused by collecting sensitive information that the group never actually needed.
The safest sensitive information is often the information a group never collects.

BOUNDARY

🔒 PRIVATE WORKSPACE DATA is intended for encrypted private storage. PUBLIC WHEN PUBLISHED means exactly that: publishing deliberately crosses the privacy boundary.
